hummm app privacy policy

Valley Road Labs Limited · Last updated 13 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the hummm app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and the choices you have. It covers the hummm app for iPhone and Android.

Who we are

The hummm app is published by Valley Road Labs Limited ("hummm", "we"), registered in Ireland (company no. 787259), 56 Raglan Lane, Dublin D04 R9T8, Ireland. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

Privacy contact: db@valleyroadlabs.com

What the app does

hummm is a home monitoring service for families and carers looking after an older relative who lives independently. Small radar sensors in the home notice when someone is in a room and moving. The app shows carers how the day is going, for example which room was last active, whether the resident has gone to bed or got up, and alerts when something looks unusual. The sensors have no cameras or microphones and cannot identify a person.

hummm is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, and it is not an emergency service. If you are worried about someone's health, contact a healthcare professional, and in an emergency call your local emergency number.

Who uses the app

App accounts are created by hummm for carers and family members. You cannot sign up inside the app. The person whose home has the sensors (the resident) does not use the app, but information about their home is shown in it, so this policy covers them too.

Room readings and health

Room presence and activity readings are not medical measurements. Because patterns such as time spent in bed or night-time trips can indirectly say something about a person's health, we protect them as special category data under the GDPR. We only process them with the resident's explicit consent, or the consent of a person legally authorised to decide for them, and we only show them to the carers the resident or their representative has chosen.

Legal bases

  • App user data: to provide the service you or your organisation signed up for (contract), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working well.
  • Resident data: explicit consent, as above. Consent can be withdrawn at any time, which stops monitoring.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers, under written terms that require them to protect the data and use it only to provide their service to us:

  • ThingsBoard Cloud (ThingsBoard, Inc.), which stores sensor readings and app accounts, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), which delivers push notifications. Apple's push notification service delivers them to iPhones.

Some of our team and contractors work outside the European Economic Area, including in South Africa and Zimbabwe, and may access personal data only to run and support the service. Where that happens we rely on the safeguards the GDPR requires for international transfers. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

How we keep it secure

Data is encrypted in transit between the app, the sensors and our servers. Access is limited to the people who need it to run the service, and app users only see the homes assigned to them.

How long we keep it

  • App account data is kept while the account is active. When an account is deleted, its personal data is deleted within 30 days.
  • Room readings are kept while the home's service is active, and deleted on request by the resident or their representative, or when the service ends, except where we must keep records for legal reasons.

Deleting your account

You can ask for your account and its data to be deleted from inside the app, in Settings, or without the app by following the steps on our account deletion page.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email db@valleyroadlabs.com and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (dataprotection.ie) or the data protection authority where you live.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

We will update this page if how we handle personal data changes, and tell app users about significant changes in the app or by email.
