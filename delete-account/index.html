Delete your hummm account

hummm app · Valley Road Labs Limited

This page explains how to delete your account in the hummm app, published by Valley Road Labs Limited, and what happens to your data.

How to ask

  1. Email db@valleyroadlabs.com from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my hummm account.
  2. If you no longer have access to that email address, tell us your name and the home you could view, and we will confirm your identity before deleting anything.

What is deleted

  • Your account, name and email address
  • Your access to the homes you could view
  • Your push notification tokens
  • Your responses to alerts, or they are made anonymous so they can no longer be linked to you

We complete deletion within 30 days of the request.

What is kept

  • Room readings from a resident's home belong to that home's service, not to your account, so they are not deleted when a carer leaves. A resident or their representative can ask for them to be deleted using the same email address.
  • Records we must keep by law, for example billing records, for as long as the law requires.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
